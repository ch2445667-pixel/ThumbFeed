Rewrite the auto-tagger and fix the facet counts it fed

The importer's tagger matched keywords with String.includes, so "ai" fired
inside "said", "mac" inside "machine" and "war" inside "warm". It also wrote
style names that do not exist in the app's VisualStyle union -- "Bold
Typography", "Product Focus", "Split Screen" -- so selecting any style in the
filter bar matched nothing at all. Its tags were just the longest words in the
title: "Gen", "Gave", "Having".

scripts/tagger.js replaces it. Matching is on word boundaries; every emitted
niche, style and emotion is validated against the app's controlled
vocabularies; tags come from a curated topic lexicon so a tag means the same
thing everywhere, with proper nouns and an ALL-CAPS hook word as fallback.
scripts/test-tagger.js covers the substring failures and asserts no value
outside the vocabularies ever gets emitted.

Two counting bugs in the facets route:

PostgREST caps responses at 1000 rows, so limit(5000) returned the first
thousand rows and every count was computed over half the data. It now pages
with range() until exhausted: "All" reads 2140, not 1000.

Counts also folded every tag into the niche counts, which is what produced
~700 phantom categories in the filter bar. Only real niche names are folded
in now, mirroring the niche-or-tag match in the gallery route.

and HimanshuG were the gaps) and re-tagged all rows with the new tagger.
import_progress.json is now gitignored -- it is local run state.
